Questions, answered

Why 10 days?+

Each day builds on the one before. Ten consecutive days give every part of the program time to work together.

Is it uncomfortable or embarrassing?+

You’re in your own private room, lying comfortably and fully covered. Water flows gently on a gravity-fed system, and you control the pace. A therapist is on call the whole time.

Do I need to fast?+

No. You’ll eat organic soups and dishes and drink veggie and green juices, all included.

What’s included in the price?+

All sessions and modalities, daily education, your take-home workbook, and your food and juices. Lodging and travel are not included.

I’ve tried cleanses before. How is this different?+

Most people have tried pieces. This brings more than 10 modalities, education and emotional support into one guided program shaped around you.

Can I keep seeing my doctor and taking my medications?+

Yes. Keep working with your healthcare provider. The retreat complements your care; it doesn’t replace it.

How many people attend each retreat?+

Only 8 guests per retreat, one retreat a month.

How do payment and refunds work?+

A $1,000 deposit reserves your dates. Full payment is due 30 days before your start date. There are no refunds after full payment. Financing is available.

Is the retreat right for me?+

That’s what the free consultation is for. It’s a no-pressure way to find out, and not every applicant is accepted.
